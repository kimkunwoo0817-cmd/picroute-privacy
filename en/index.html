Pathpin Privacy Policy

Effective: November 2, 2026 · This is the English version of the privacy policy for Pathpin (called 픽루트, PicRoute, in Korea). It is a translation of the Korean policy (픽루트 개인정보 처리방침). If the two versions ever differ, the Korean version prevails, unless the law of your country provides otherwise.

WATSI ("the Company", "we") publishes this privacy policy for the mobile application Pathpin ("the Service") under Article 30 of the Korean Personal Information Protection Act, so that we protect your personal information and handle your concerns smoothly. We process only the minimum personal information needed for each purpose.

Summary
  • The photos, location coordinates, memos and walked tracks you record in Pathpin are stored on your phone and are not sent to us. We cannot see or keep these records.
  • There is no sign-up or login, and we have not added any advertising or behavioral-analytics tools. However, the app's update check produces usage statistics such as the number of installs (Section 11).
  • The personal information we process is: (1) what you give us when you contact us or ask for a refund; (2) the sales records that the app stores provide to sellers; (3) technical information (install ID, IP address, etc.) that Expo processes for us to check for app updates; (4) if you choose the trail map, technical information (IP address, the map area you are viewing, etc.) that Tracestrack processes for us to deliver trail-map images (Sections 1 and 5).
  • Separately, when maps are shown (Google; on iPhone, Apple as the base map under the trail map) and when you pay (the app stores), information such as device details, your IP address and the map area you are viewing goes directly to those companies (Section 4).
  • If you live outside Korea (for example in the EU/EEA, the UK, Japan or the United States), Section 16 gives additional information required by the laws of those places.

2. Information processed only on your phone

The following information is stored in the app's storage on your phone and is not sent to any server of ours (we do not operate servers).

  • Photo entries: the coordinates (latitude and longitude) where you took the photo or of the point you picked on the map, the time recorded, the sequence number, the title and memo you entered, and the gallery ID and file name of the linked photo
  • Walked tracks while walk recording is on: for each point, the coordinates (latitude, longitude and altitude), the margin of error of the position and altitude, speed and direction, and the time recorded; for each segment, its start and end time and the distance walked
  • Trip names, their start and end times, and memo templates
  • Trips, entries, walked tracks and planned routes (routes drawn in advance in another app) read from files you import (KML, GPX, CSV, or a ZIP containing them). Imported files are read only on your phone.
  • A temporary copy of what you were writing on the entry screen (location coordinates and time, trip name, title and memo, and the file location of the photo you picked): kept so that you can continue if the app closes unexpectedly. It is deleted when you save or close the screen, and a copy older than one day is deleted the next time you open the app.
  • Trail-map images received while viewing the trail map: kept in your phone's temporary storage so that they need not be downloaded again; images older than 30 days are downloaded again (your phone may delete them if storage runs low).
  • App settings, the start date of the free trial, and the subscription status last checked

Photos you take, and copies of photos you pick from your albums to add to entries, are saved in the "Pathpin" album of your phone's Gallery (Photos app on iPhone). (If your phone was set to Korean when you started using the app, or you were already using it before the overseas version, the album is called "픽루트". If saving to the album is not possible, the photo is saved in the Gallery's default photo folder.) The original photos in your albums are left as they are.

Deletion: You can delete entries in the app, and they are deleted from your phone when you delete the app or clear its data. However, the following may remain:

  1. Photos in your Gallery: they remain even if you delete the app, and you can delete them in your photos app.
  2. Files you have exported (KML, GPX, CSV, ZIP, and photo bundles): they remain until you delete them. A photo bundle made for sharing stays briefly in your phone's temporary storage and is deleted the next time you make a photo bundle or open the app.
  3. Phone backups: the Service is set to allow the operating system to back up the app. If your phone's backup is on, the app's entries and settings may be backed up to your own account. On Android, if Google backup is on and the app data is within the backup limit, it may be restored when you reinstall the app; on iPhone, it may be restored when you restore the whole phone from an iCloud backup. We cannot see these backups, and you can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

  • You can turn off any permission at any time in your phone's Settings > Apps > Pathpin > Permissions (on iPhone, Settings > Pathpin).

We do not provide the personal information in Section 1 to third parties. Only where a law specifically requires it (for example, a request under a warrant issued by a court), or where it is clearly necessary to protect the life, physical safety or property of you or another person from imminent danger, do we provide it, and then only to the extent allowed by PIPA Articles 17 and 18. What the Google Maps component built into the app sends directly to Google is described in Section 4(1), and what goes directly to Apple when you choose the trail map on iPhone is described in Section 4(4). Sending requests to Tracestrack for trail-map images is processing on our behalf, not disclosure to a third party (Section 5(3)).

7. Deletion

  1. We delete personal information without delay when its retention period ends or its purpose has been achieved.
  2. Information that must be kept by law (the basis, items and periods are in the table in Section 1) is moved to a separate mailbox or folder so that it is not mixed with other inquiries.
  3. Procedure: the privacy officer regularly selects inquiry emails whose retention period has passed and deletes them. Sales records in the app stores are deleted from the store accounts when their retention period ends, or, where they cannot be deleted, are no longer viewed.
  4. Method: emails are deleted from the mailbox and the trash, and electronic files are deleted in a way that cannot be recovered.

8. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14. If, while handling an inquiry, we learn that information belongs to a child under 14, we delete it without delay unless the law requires us to keep it.

10. Security

  1. Only the privacy officer can access the personal information we process.
  2. Records in the app are not stored on any server of ours, so we cannot access them.
  3. App update checks use an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
  4. Inquiry emails and sales records are viewed in password-protected company accounts, and we do not download and keep sales reports as files.

11. Automatic collection

The Service does not use cookies, does not read or use advertising identifiers, and has no advertising or behavioral-analytics tools added. However, the following two create a random identifier on your phone and send it to outside servers.

  • The install ID for app update checks (Expo) (Section 5(1)): a new one is created if you delete and reinstall the app or clear its data (if you restore from a phone backup, the previous ID may carry over). Expo counts update-check requests and shows us statistics such as the number of installs.
  • The pseudonymous identifier of the Google Maps component (Section 4(1)): handled under Google's policies.

Neither is sent if you turn off your internet connection.

12. Information we do not process

We do not process sensitive information (such as health or beliefs, as defined by law), unique identification information (such as resident registration numbers) or pseudonymized information, and we make no decisions that affect you based solely on automated processing without human involvement.
