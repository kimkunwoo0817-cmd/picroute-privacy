Article 1 (Purpose)

These terms set out the rights, obligations and responsibilities of WATSI ("the Company", "we") and users regarding the use of the mobile application Pathpin (called 픽루트, PicRoute, in Korea) ("the Service").

Article 2 (Definitions)

  1. (1) "Service" means the application the Company provides so that you can take photos on site (or add photos from your albums) or pick points on a map, record the location, time and memo, record the path you walk, and export them as files or import files you have received to view them.
  2. (2) "User" ("you") means a person who uses the Service under these terms.
  3. (3) "Records" means the location coordinates, times, titles and memos, photo link information (the number and file name pointing to a photo in your gallery) and walked tracks you create with the Service, the points and routes read from files you import, and information attached to them. In the app, each recorded point is called an "entry".
  4. (4) A "trip" is the unit that groups records (for example, one day of fieldwork). "Continue a trip" is the feature that reopens a trip you have already ended to add records to it.
  5. (5) "App store" means the place where you download and pay for the Service, such as Google Play or the Apple App Store.
  6. (6) "Subscription" means a paid, fixed-term right of use that is purchased through an app store and renews automatically.
  7. (7) "Free trial" means the period under Article 7 during which you can use every feature without paying.

Article 4 (What the Service provides)

  1. (1) The Company provides the following:
    1. Recording location coordinates, time, sequence number, title and memo together with a photo you take (or pick from your albums), or at a point you pick on the map
    2. Viewing records on a map (the standard and satellite maps and, where the Company provides it, the trail map) and correcting their positions
    3. Recording the path you walk while walk recording is on
    4. Exporting records as KML, GPX or CSV files (or a ZIP containing them), and exporting a trip's photos as they are in a ZIP file ("photo bundle")
    5. Importing KML, GPX or CSV files (or a ZIP containing them) you have received and viewing them as trips
    6. Other features the Company adds

However, if it removes or substantially reduces a major feature provided under the subscription, it gives notice on the terms web page and in the app 30 days in advance and, if you wish, refunds the fee for the remaining period of use (calculated as in Article 12(6)).

Article 5 (Storage of records and responsibility)

  1. (1) Your records are stored on your phone and are not sent to any server of the Company. The Company runs no server and does not receive or keep your photos, location coordinates, memos or walked tracks.
  2. (2) Therefore the Company cannot access your records and cannot restore them for you. If records are lost because the phone is lost, broken or reset, the app is deleted, or through your own actions, the Company cannot recover them.
  3. (3) Please keep important records separately using the Service's export feature.
  4. (4) The Service is set to allow the phone's operating system to back up the app. If your phone's backup is on, your records may be backed up to your own account. On Android, if Google backup is on and the app data is within the backup limit, they may be restored when you reinstall the app; on iPhone, they may be restored when you restore the whole phone from an iCloud backup (reinstalling only the app does not restore them). This backup takes place between you and the operating-system provider, and the Company cannot see what is backed up. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.
  5. (5) The rights to your records belong to you, and the Company claims no rights over them.
